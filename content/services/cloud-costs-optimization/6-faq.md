## FAQ

{{< details summary="How much does it cost?" >}}
  We start with a free assessment to determine whether there’s significant
  potential for savings. We then charge by the hour and provide regular
  progress updates, so you stay in control of how far we go. In most cases,
  the savings cover the cost of our cloud optimization work within a few months.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Will there be any downtime when we implement the optimizations?" >}}
  Most likely not. We have extensive experience updating production
  infrastructure without downtime. In any case, we'll put together a plan and
  coordinate with you before making any changes.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How much involvement will you need from our engineering team?" >}}
  The initial assessment requires minimal involvement, typically one or two
  meetings with your technical lead. As we move into reviewing the proposed
  optimization plan, we'll need closer collaboration with your engineering
  team.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How soon can you start?" >}}
  Free consultations are typically available within one business day. Once the
  paperwork is complete and we have system access, we begin the initial
  assessment right away.
{{< /details >}}
