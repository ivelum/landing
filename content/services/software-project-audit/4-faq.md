## FAQ

{{< details summary="What do I get after the audit?" >}}
  You’ll receive an actionable report with findings and concrete
  recommendations for the areas we reviewed. We’ll discuss the results with
  you and help you decide what to tackle next.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Can you run an audit that focuses on just one area, such as security?" >}}
  Absolutely. That’s the point—you tell us what you’d like to focus on, and
  we’ll focus on exactly that. If you want a security-only audit, we'll do that.
  If you'd prefer a bit of everything, we can do that too.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="What access do you need to run the audit?" >}}
  We’ll agree on the access needed based on your chosen focus areas. This may
  include read-only access to your code, infrastructure, or project
  documentation. We sign an NDA and contract before accessing your systems.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Can you help us implement the recommendations?" >}}
  Yes. We can help you address the issues identified during the audit. Any
  follow-up work is scoped and agreed separately, so you can choose what to
  handle with your team and where you’d like our support.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How soon can you start?" >}}
  We can usually schedule a free discovery call within one business day and
  start work once the paperwork is complete and we've been granted access to
  the system under audit.
{{< /details >}}
