## Best practices

There’s plenty of advice online about reducing cloud costs. It typically comes
down to the following:

- Analyze spending, attribute costs to teams, and configure budgets and alerts.
- Remove redundant resources and automatically shut down unused environments.
- Scale down underutilized instances, evaluate different instance types.
- Review backup retention policies and choose the most cost-effective storage
  for each use case.
- Purchase savings plans and reserved instances, and consider using spot
  instances where applicable.
- Explore services from other cloud providers.

All of these practices make perfect sense, and they’re an important part of our
cloud optimization work. But there’s an opportunity to go further.
