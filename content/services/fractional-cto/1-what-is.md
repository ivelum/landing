## What is Fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is a part-time technology leader who performs the same core
responsibilities as a full-time CTO, including leading engineering teams,
shaping technology strategy, and optimizing costs, just without the full-time
commitment.

Depending on where you look, the company size, and the level of involvement,
Fractional CTO services may cost anywhere from $3,000 to $25,000 per month.
We provide this service at no additional cost to all of our clients.
