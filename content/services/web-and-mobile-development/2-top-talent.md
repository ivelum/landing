## Top 2% talent

Finding highly qualified full-stack developers has never been easy. We know
this firsthand: more than ten years ago, we decided to focus on full-stack
development, and it has been our core strategy ever since.

Our hiring data shows that only about 1 in 50 developers who meet the formal
job requirements make it from the first interview to an offer, so it remains
hard even today. But our clients' projects show us clearly that it's worth
the effort, which is why we keep going the extra mile to find the best
full-stack developers.
