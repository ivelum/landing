## FAQ

{{< details summary="Do you need detailed requirements?" >}}
  Not at all. Just share your idea, and we'll brainstorm the best approach
  together. If you've already done some research or built a working prototype,
  great: we'll build on what you have.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Can you work with a prototype built with AI or no-code tools?" >}}
  Absolutely. We’ll review what you already have, figure out what can be reused
  and what is missing, and take it from there.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Will the MVP need to be rebuilt if the product takes off?" >}}
  Not necessarily. We build on proven [technologies](/technology/) and
  architecture that can scale without being overengineered, so even the first
  release gives you a solid foundation to grow from. Some parts may need to
  evolve as usage increases, but an MVP doesn’t have to be disposable.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Who owns the code?" >}}
  You do. As our customer, you own all intellectual property developed under
  the contract. All service accounts, such as GitHub, AWS, Sentry, and others,
  are registered in your company's name. All work is carried out in code
  repositories and production environments that you own.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Do I need someone technical to manage your developers?" >}}
  Not necessarily. If you have a CTO or technical co-founder, we’re happy to
  work with them. If you don’t, we offer
  [Fractional CTO services at no additional cost](/services/fractional-cto/)
  to all our clients.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How much of my time will you need?" >}}
  It depends on the project, but most of our clients find that a weekly
  check-in works well. If anything comes up between check-ins, we can have a
  quick chat or a call. We also post daily updates in the team chat, so you
  can follow our progress whenever it suits you.
{{< /details >}}
