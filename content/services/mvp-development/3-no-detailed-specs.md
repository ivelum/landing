## No need for detailed specs

Some agencies expect you to know exactly what you want to build before they
start. We don't. Even if your idea isn't fully formed yet, we'll brainstorm it
with you and suggest the best way to build it. We'll research the competition,
map out user flows, create the designs, and provide
[strategic technology guidance](/services/fractional-cto/) along the way.

Already have a prototype? Great, we'll take it from there. We'll build what's
missing and take care of quality, security, and production infrastructure, so
your launch makes the right first impression.
