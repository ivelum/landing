## Likely weeks, under $10k

In our experience, an MVP for most software product ideas can be launched
within a few weeks. Why not days, you might ask? Shouldn't that be possible
with AI? It may be, but a product built that quickly will likely have plenty
of rough edges, and competition among startups is now at an all-time high. And
if something can be built that fast, it can be copied just as fast, or even
faster. On the other hand, spending months building an MVP before getting any
user feedback is risky. That's why we see "a few weeks" as the sweet spot.
